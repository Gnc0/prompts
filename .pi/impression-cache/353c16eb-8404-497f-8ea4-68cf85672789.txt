- **Publication/source kind:** arXiv preprint

## 3. AgentCoder: Multi-Agent-based Code Generation with Iterative Testing and Optimisation
- **Original URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.13010
- **Publication/source kind:** arXiv preprint
- **Exact abstract/result passage:** “AgentCoder (GPT-4) achieves 96.3% and 91.8% pass@1 in HumanEval and MBPP datasets with an overall token overhead of 56.9K and 66.3K, while state-of-the-art obtains only 90.2% and 78.9% pass@1 with an overall token overhead of 138.2K and 206.5K.”
- **Minimal faithful summary / study scope:** Evaluates programmer, test-designer, and test-executor agents on HumanEval and MBPP; reports pass@1 and aggregate token overhead.

- **Original URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02172
- **Publication/source kind:** arXiv preprint
- **Exact abstract passage:** “CodeAgent incorporates a supervisory agent, QA-Checker, to ensure that all the agents' contributions address the initial review question.”
- **Minimal faithful summary / study scope:** Evaluates a supervisory multi-agent code-review system on change/message consistency, vulnerability introduction, style adherence, and revision suggestion.
